03 / LEARN & UNDERSTAND

Algorithm insights

Understand why two correct solutions can have very different performance.

APPROACH 01

Naive traversal

O(n²)

For each incoming record, the algorithm may scan the existing consolidated list. As unique products increase, more comparisons may be needed.

Simple approach · Can scale poorly
APPROACH 02

Dictionary mapping

O(n) average

A dictionary maps each product ID to its price and quantity. Lookups and updates take constant time on average.

Efficient lookups · Better for larger carts

Complexity describes how runtime grows with input size. Actual benchmark timings depend on the dataset, computer, and runtime environment.
